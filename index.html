About

I build the whole thing - the pitch, the funnel and the product.

I'm Jordan - a creative technologist and founder based in Aba, Nigeria. I run a small studio that helps ecommerce brands look sharp and sell more, and I build software products of my own.

Most people pick a lane: strategy or design or code. I never did. I started in ad creative and media buying, learned to build the storefronts the ads pointed at, and kept going until I could ship the whole product. That range is the point - fewer handoffs, faster loops, one person accountable for the result.

For founders, that means a campaign, a funnel and a store that actually convert, built by someone who understands the ad account and the codebase. For engineers and product people, it means proof I can ship: a multi-tenant SaaS, an AI video studio, and eight live client builds.

I work solo, with AI assistance, and I ship monthly. No agency telephone game, no nonsense - always on the cutting edge of what's possible for a small team.
