You're not lazy. You're doing six jobs.

The hours table that shocks solo owners - and the difference between a chatbot you ask and a staff member who shows up.

Jordan Nwachukwu Marketer · creative technologist · Pixelhd Studios, Lagos

Let's be honest about your week. Content creator: ideas, photos, captions, reels, Status updates - 8 to 12 hours. Social media manager: posting on three or four platforms, replying to comments - 5 to 8. Ad person: writing ads, making creatives, checking results - 3 to 6. Customer service: "How much?", "Do you deliver to Ibadan?", "Is it original?" - 10 to 15. Sales closer: follow-ups, chasing payments, objections - 4 to 8. Operations: stock, packing, dispatch, suppliers, records - 10 to 20.

Add it up. That is often 40 to 70 hours a week of work that is not the thing you are actually good at: making your product or serving your customers. The hours are a rough guide; write down your own numbers. Most owners are shocked by the total.

The old fix doesn't fit

You cannot afford six salaries. And the tool everyone recommends - ChatGPT - forgets you every new chat. It only answers when you ask. It lives in one website, gives the same help to everybody, and remains one general assistant. Think of a chatbot as a smart stranger you ask questions.

An AI agent is a staff member who knows your business and shows up every morning.

An agent remembers your business, prices, customers and style. It works on a schedule and messages you first. It lives in Telegram and WhatsApp, where you already are. It gets more tailored the more you use it. And it can be a team of specialists, each with its own job - not one generalist.

Six names, six jobs

The team in the book is deliberately ordinary: Ngozi, chief of staff, sends a daily briefing before you open your eyes. Ada, content strategist, plans a month of content in one sitting. Tolu, social media manager, writes captions and posts in your voice. Kemi, ad creative writer, drafts ads and scripts. Chidi, sales follow-up officer, never lets a warm lead go cold. Musa, group moderator, keeps your community warm and clean.

They run on Hermes, a free open-source desktop agent - install in 20 minutes, click through setup, no terminal - with a "Business Brain" you teach your prices, products and rules. Customer-facing chats can start even sooner: Meta Business Agent is built into WhatsApp Business and answers "How much?" at 11pm. Meta is rolling it out market by market, and everything else works without it.

The golden rule

AI prepares, you approve. You stay in charge of anything involving money - ad spend, refunds, discounts - anything posted publicly in your name, and your relationships with big customers and suppliers. Your AI staff draft. You decide. And the honest limits stay on the table: free tiers mean light daily use with limits, and your laptop must be on for staff to work.

That is the whole pitch: stop being the whole company. Hire six AI staff this weekend. Pay ₦0 salary. The weekend schedule, the job descriptions and the prompt pack are in the guide.
